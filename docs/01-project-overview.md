# 🚀 Project Overview

## Project Name

**TrendPilot AI**

## Project Type

AI-Powered Content Intelligence SaaS

---

# Vision

TrendPilot AI is an AI-powered Content Intelligence Platform that helps marketing and growth teams discover, analyze, and transform industry trends into actionable content opportunities.

Instead of spending hours manually researching blogs, YouTube channels, and industry news, TrendPilot AI continuously monitors selected sources, analyzes newly published content using AI, and recommends the next high-impact content ideas through a centralized dashboard.

---

# Problem Statement

Marketing and growth teams spend significant time manually researching blogs, YouTube channels, competitors, and industry websites to decide what content should be created next.

This traditional workflow is:

- Time-consuming
- Repetitive
- Difficult to scale
- Based on assumptions instead of data
- Inconsistent across different team members

As a result, valuable opportunities are often missed, and content planning becomes inefficient.

---

# Proposed Solution

TrendPilot AI automates the entire content research workflow.

Users can add their preferred websites and YouTube channels as monitoring sources. Every 4–6 hours, the platform automatically checks for newly published blogs and videos, extracts meaningful information, and uses Google Gemini AI to generate structured insights.

Instead of simply displaying summaries, the platform converts each piece of content into actionable recommendations, including trending topics, content ideas, hooks, outlines, keywords, and publishing suggestions for social media platforms.

---

# Key Objectives

- Reduce manual content research time by more than 80%
- Automate website and YouTube monitoring
- Generate AI-powered summaries and topic extraction
- Discover emerging industry trends automatically
- Recommend high-quality content ideas for social media
- Centralize all research inside one dashboard
- Build a scalable SaaS platform using the MERN stack
